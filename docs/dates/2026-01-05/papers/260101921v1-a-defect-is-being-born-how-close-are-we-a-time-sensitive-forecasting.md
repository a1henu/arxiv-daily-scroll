# A Defect is Being Born: How Close Are We? A Time Sensitive Forecasting Approach
**arXiv**：[2601.01921v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.01921) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2601.01921.pdf)  
**作者**：Mikel Robredo, Matteo Esposito, Fabio Palomba, Rafael Peñaloza, Valentina Lenarduzzi  

**一句话要点**：提出时间敏感预测方法以解决软件缺陷早期预测问题

**关键词**：缺陷预测, 时间敏感预测, 软件工程, 早期症状识别, 实证研究

## 3 点简述
- 核心问题：软件系统持续演化，需时间敏感方法预测缺陷发生前兆
- 方法要点：训练多种时间敏感预测技术，预测未来缺陷密度并识别早期症状
- 实验或效果：预期提供实证证据，评估方法在早期缺陷倾向估计中的有效性

## 摘要（原文）

> Background. Defect prediction has been a highly active topic among researchers in the Empirical Software Engineering field. Previous literature has successfully achieved the most accurate prediction of an incoming fault and identified the features and anomalies that precede it through just-in-time prediction. As software systems evolve continuously, there is a growing need for time-sensitive methods capable of forecasting defects before they manifest.
>   Aim. Our study seeks to explore the effectiveness of time-sensitive techniques for defect forecasting. Moreover, we aim to investigate the early indicators that precede the occurrence of a defect.
>   Method. We will train multiple time-sensitive forecasting techniques to forecast the future bug density of a software project, as well as identify the early symptoms preceding the occurrence of a defect.
>   Expected results. Our expected results are translated into empirical evidence on the effectiveness of our approach for early estimation of bug proneness.
