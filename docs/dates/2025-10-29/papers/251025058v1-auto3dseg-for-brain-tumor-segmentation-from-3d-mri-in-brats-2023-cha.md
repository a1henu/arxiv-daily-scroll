# Auto3DSeg for Brain Tumor Segmentation from 3D MRI in BraTS 2023 Challenge
**arXiv**：[2510.25058v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.25058) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2510.25058.pdf)  
**作者**：Andriy Myronenko, Dong Yang, Yufan He, Daguang Xu  

**一句话要点**：提出Auto3DSeg方法用于BraTS 2023挑战中的脑肿瘤3D MRI分割

**关键词**：脑肿瘤分割, 3D MRI, Auto3DSeg, MONAI框架, BraTS挑战

## 3 点简述
- 核心问题：解决脑肿瘤在3D MRI图像中的自动分割挑战。
- 方法要点：基于MONAI框架的Auto3DSeg工具进行分割。
- 实验或效果：在BraTS 2023五个挑战中获三项第一、两项第二。

## 摘要（原文）

> In this work, we describe our solution to the BraTS 2023 cluster of
> challenges using Auto3DSeg from MONAI. We participated in all 5 segmentation
> challenges, and achieved the 1st place results in three of them: Brain
> Metastasis, Brain Meningioma, BraTS-Africa challenges, and the 2nd place
> results in the remaining two: Adult and Pediatic Glioma challenges.
