# First Proof
**arXiv**：[2602.05192v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.05192) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2602.05192.pdf)  
**作者**：Mohammed Abouzaid, Andrew J. Blumberg, Martin Hairer, Joe Kileel, Tamara G. Kolda, Paul D. Nelson, Daniel Spielman, Nikhil Srivastava, Rachel Ward, Shmuel Weinberger, Lauren Williams  

**一句话要点**：提出十道未公开数学问题以评估AI系统研究级数学能力

**关键词**：AI评估, 数学问题, 研究级测试, 未公开数据, 加密答案

## 3 点简述
- 核心问题：评估当前AI系统在回答研究级数学问题上的准确性
- 方法要点：作者基于自身研究过程设计十道未公开数学问题
- 实验或效果：答案已知但暂时加密，用于测试AI系统性能

## 摘要（原文）

> To assess the ability of current AI systems to correctly answer research-level mathematics questions, we share a set of ten math questions which have arisen naturally in the research process of the authors. The questions had not been shared publicly until now; the answers are known to the authors of the questions but will remain encrypted for a short time.
